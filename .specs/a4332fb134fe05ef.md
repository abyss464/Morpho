# Today ViewModel

Reads the stream as of now and derives everything Today shows.

## TodayEntry (enum)

What the Today button opens the stream as: START (nothing done today), CONTINUE (steps were taken today or one is waiting on screen), SUMMARY (nothing left today; the stream opens on its done screen).

## UnitProgress

One unit: its number, its size (20, fewer for the last), and how many of its words are graduated (have an FSRS card). `finished` when all are.

## TodayUiState

- **loading** — true until the first refresh lands
- **greetingPeriod**, **streakDays** — header
- **dueReviews**, **newWords** — the Today line: due reviews now, and new words still allowed today (zero while reviews are at the backlog guard)
- **backlogged** — due reviews reached the backlog guard
- **entry** — the button's label and meaning
- **journey** — words in the release, words met (cards in this release plus Learning/Relearning words), words being learned
- **estimatedDaysRemaining** — at the recent pace of new words
- **weeklyActivity**, **heatmapData**, **activityChartStyle** — this week
- **units** — up to eight units starting two before the first unfinished one; **unitsFinished**, **unitCount**

## Operations

### refresh()
Opens the stream snapshot (learning order, cards, state), rolls the day forward, and recomputes every figure.

### setActivityChartStyle(style)
Switches the activity chart and persists the choice.
