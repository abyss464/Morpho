# Headword Mask

Full-sentence learner definitions name the word they define ("If you abandon something, you leave it..."). Wherever the learner still has to pick or recall the word, that sentence would give the answer away, so the headword is blanked out there. The forms and the word-boundary rule are the engine's self-reference check (`inflections` and `contains_whole_word` in `core/crates/reconcile/src/score.rs`), so a definition the engine reads as naming its own headword is exactly one this masks.

## HEADWORD_MASK

The replacement text: four underscores (`____`).

## maskHeadword(lemma, text) -> masked text

Replaces every whole-word, case-insensitive occurrence of the lemma or one of its regular inflections in the text with HEADWORD_MASK. Punctuation, spacing and the rest of the text are kept. Returns the text unchanged when nothing matches or the lemma is blank.

- Lemma folding: NFC, trimmed, internal whitespace collapsed to one space, lowercased.
- Forms: the lemma, +s, +es, +ed, +d, +ing; consonant+y gives ies / ied; final e gives stem+ing / stem+ed; a consonant after a single vowel after a consonant doubles before ing / ed, except w, x and y. Irregular forms are not generated.
- Whole word: a word character is an ASCII letter or digit, or any non-ASCII character. "resourceful" is not masked for "resource"; "self-abandon" and "abandon's" are masked for "abandon".
- Where several forms start at the same position, the longest one that ends on a word boundary is masked.
