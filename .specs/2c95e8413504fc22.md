# Word Screen

A word opened from "Look up a word": the full word card, read only.

## LookedUp
The word's bundle, its unit, whether it has an FSRS card, and the learner's note.

## WordViewModel(container, wordId)
Loads the word. Reads only: looking a word up never changes the stream. `readAloud(word)` plays word, definition and example as one run.

## WordScreen(container, wordId, onBack, modifier)
A back button and the word card (picture, "Unit N" or "In review · Unit N", word, phonetic, part of speech, the play-all speaker, definition, example, note). Audio stops on leaving.
