# Stream ViewModel

Drives the stream: asks the StreamEngine for the next step, loads what the step shows, runs its task, and writes every finished step (cards, the day's counts, the stream state) before choosing the next. The step on screen is saved as the state's `current`, so leaving and resuming lands on it.

## Task states

- **RebuildState** — the explain task: the puzzle, the pieces placed in the tray, whether the tray was checked, failed checks (`misses`), whether a hint was used, solved. `right(k)` tells whether tray slot k holds the right piece; `wrongShown` when a full tray was checked and is wrong.
- **FillState** — the use task: the gap, the four options (the word and its three bound distractors, shuffled by the word id + 7), the answer id, the wrongly picked ids, solved.

## ReviewVerdict

A solved review: its outcome, the rating applied, and the days each rating would schedule from the card as it was before the review.

## StepView

The step on screen: the step, the word, its unit, whether a `know` step is for a word already being learned ("Look again"), the word's note, its review count, the task state, whether the review picture is shown, the outcome once solved, the verdict once a review is solved, and a serial that changes with every step load.

## DoneSummary

Steps, reviewed (and clean), met (and met clean), reviews due by the end of tomorrow, new words a day, and the streak.

## StreamUiState

Steps done and the estimated total (engine progress), the step view or the done summary, and the audio file playing now.

## Behaviour

- On start it opens the snapshot and shows the saved step or the next one; with no step left it shows the done summary (and plays the review-done sound when arriving from a step).
- Decoy pools: `explain1` uses the words being learned plus the eight met words latest in learning order (easy pieces); `explain2` and review rebuilds use twelve met words chosen by shuffling met ids with the word's id (hard pieces).
- Audio: a `know` step reads word, definition and example as one run; a rebuild review plays the word; a fill review stays silent. Solving an explain reads the definition; a right fill reads the example.
- Feedback: CORRECT/WRONG sound and haptics on checks and picks, TAP on presses, PROMOTE when Continue graduates a word.
- A step whose word cannot be loaded leaves the stream unrated; a word failing twice ends the day's stream.

## Operations

- **onPlacePiece(id)** / **onReturnPiece(id)** / **onStartOver()** — move pieces; placing the last piece checks the tray
- **onShowNextPiece()** — keeps the correct opening pieces and places the next correct one (counts as a hint)
- **onPickWord(wordId)** — picks a fill option
- **onShowPicture()** — uncovers a review's picture
- **onContinue()** — finishes a learning step (always allowed on `know`, otherwise once solved), or leaves a review result
- **onRate(grade)** — replaces a review's derived rating; the card is rescheduled from its state before the review
- **onSaveNote(text)** — saves the word's note
- **onMeetMore()** — raises today's new-word allowance by 5 and goes back into the stream
- **readAloud(word)**, **onPlayWord(word)** — audio
- **onPause()** — stops audio (the step stays saved)
