# Sync Document

The version-1 progress document of docs/contracts/sync.md §2, as kotlinx.serialization classes. Field names are the JSON keys.

## SyncDocument

- **v** — the document version, 1 (`VERSION`)
- **words** — word id (as a string) to SyncEntry
- **notes** — word id (as a string) to SyncNote

## SyncEntry

A word's progress: its card and its stage, each nullable. A word on neither side is absent from the document.

## SyncCard

The FSRS card of a graduated word: `due`, `stability`, `difficulty`, `elapsedDays`, `scheduledDays`, `reps`, `lapses`, `state` (0 New, 1 Learning, 2 Review, 3 Relearning) and `lastReview` (nullable). `due` and `lastReview` are ISO-8601 UTC strings.

## SyncStage

A word being learned or relearned in the stream: `stage` (`learning` | `relearning`), `next` (`know` | `explain1` | `explain2` | `use`), `immediate`, `needClean`, `thenUse`, `flawed`. Stream positions are per client and not part of it.

## SyncNote

The learner's note on a word: `text` and `at` (ISO-8601 UTC).
