# Stream Engine

The stream (docs/contracts/stream.md): one mixed sequence of steps that takes every word from first sight to long-term review. The web client implements the same contract in `web/src/stream.ts`.

## Types

- **StepKind** — KNOW, EXPLAIN1, EXPLAIN2, USE, REVIEW
- **ReviewTask** — REBUILD (an explain2-style rebuild) or FILL (a use-style fill-in)
- **Outcome** — CLEAN, SHAKY, FAILED
- **Stage** — LEARNING (met, not graduated) or RELEARNING (a review was rated Again)
- **WordStage** — a Learning or Relearning word: its stage, its next step, whether that step follows at once, the stream position it was scheduled at, the clean explain2 results still needed, whether `use` follows, and whether any step was shaky or failed (graduation then rates Hard)
- **StreamDay** — today's counters: date, words introduced, extra allowance, steps, reviewed (and clean), met (and clean), and the position in the review-review-new rhythm
- **Step** — a word, a step kind, and for reviews the task
- **LastReview** — the last review's word, its card before the review, and the rating applied
- **StreamState** — Learning/Relearning words, the stream position, the last three step kinds, the last word, today's counters, the step on screen, and the last review
- **StepResult** — the new state, the cards to write, and whether the step graduated a word, was a review, was graded, and was clean

## StreamEngine(fsrs)

Constants: window W = 5, spacing S = 3, backlog guard B = 50; a rebuild review under 10 s or a fill-in under 4 s rates Easy when clean.

### today(state, date)
The state with today's counters reset when the date changed.

### dueReviews(state, cards, shipped, now) -> word ids
Due cards of shipped words not being learned, the least retrievable first.

### nextNewWord(state, order, cards) / newAllowance(state, newPerDay)
The next word in learning order with no card and not in the stream; new words still allowed today (N + extra − introduced).

### next(state, order, cards, newPerDay, now, date) -> Step or null
Chooses the next step (contract §5): an immediate step first; then delayed steps spaced at least S steps; then up to two due reviews and one new word in rhythm (a new word only below the window, within the allowance and under the backlog guard); then delayed steps not yet spaced. Never the same word twice in a row, never four steps of one kind in a row, when an alternative exists. Null when today's stream is done.

### progress(state, order, cards, newPerDay, now, date) -> (done, total)
Steps done today and the estimate: done + due reviews + pending steps of Learning/Relearning words + 4 × new words still allowed.

### deriveGrade(outcome, elapsedMs, task) -> Grade
Failed is Again, shaky Hard, clean Good, or Easy under the task's time.

### complete(state, step, outcome, elapsedMs, cards, now, date) -> StepResult
Applies a finished step by the transitions of contract §3. A clean `use` graduates the word with its first card (Good, or Hard when flawed); a review reschedules its card with the derived rating, and Again turns the word Relearning.

### override(state, grade, now) -> StepResult
Replaces the last review's rating: the card is rescheduled from its state before the review; Again makes the word Relearning, another rating ends Relearning.

### intervals(prev, now) -> days per grade
The days until the next review for each rating, from the card before the review.
