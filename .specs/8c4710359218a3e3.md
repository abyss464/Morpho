# Stream ViewModel

Drives the stream: asks the StreamEngine for the next step, loads what the step shows, runs its task, and writes every finished step (cards, the day's counts, the stream state) before choosing the next. The step on screen is saved as the state's `current`, so leaving and resuming lands on it.

## Task states

- **RebuildState** — the explain task: the puzzle, per blank the piece in it or none, whether the tray was checked, failed checks (`misses`), whether help was used, solved. `right(k)` tells whether blank k holds a piece with the right text; `nextOpen` is the first empty blank; `wrongShown` when a full tray was checked and is wrong; `pieceFor(k)` is the piece that belongs in blank k.
- **SpellState** — the spelling: the puzzle, per blank the tile in it or none, checked, misses, help used, solved; `right(k)`, `nextOpen`, `wrongShown`, `tileFor(k)` and `answerFill()` (every blank with its right letter).
- **FillState** — the use task: the gap, the four options (the word and its three bound distractors, shuffled by the word id + 7), the answer id, the wrongly picked ids, solved.

## ReviewVerdict / ReviewParts / RebuiltPart

A solved review: its outcome, the rating applied, the days each rating would schedule from the card as it was before the review, and for a rebuild review how its rebuild and spelling went. RebuiltPart carries the rebuild's outcome and time into the spelling.

## StepView

The step on screen: the step, the word, its unit, whether a `know` step is for a word already being learned ("Look again"), the word's note, its review count, the task state, whether the review picture is shown, the outcome once solved, the verdict once a review is solved, and a serial that changes with every step load.

## DoneSummary

Steps, reviewed (and clean), met (and met clean), reviews due by the end of tomorrow, new words a day, the streak, and the units finished today.

## UnitNews / UnitEnding

Units finished today, in order — a unit is finished when one of its words graduated today (its card has one review, made today) and every word in it has a card — and what follows the last: the next unit starts tomorrow (none of its words has a card or is in the stream), has already begun, or there is none (it was the last unit).

## StreamUiState

Steps done and the estimated total (engine progress), the step view or the done summary, and the audio file playing now.

## Behaviour

- On start it opens the snapshot and shows the saved step or the next one; with no step left it shows the done summary (and plays the review-done sound when arriving from a step).
- Decoy pools: `explain1` uses the words being learned plus the eight met words latest in learning order (easy pieces); `explain2` and review rebuilds use twelve met words chosen by shuffling met ids with the word's id (hard pieces).
- Audio: a `know` step reads word, definition and example as one run; a rebuild review plays the word; a fill review stays silent. Solving an explain reads the definition; a right fill reads the example.
- Feedback: CORRECT/WRONG sound and haptics on checks and picks (a tray solved with help gets a tap), the tap sound and tick (`AppContainer.tap`) on every other press, PROMOTE when Continue graduates a word.
- A rebuild review is recorded once, after spelling: its outcome is the worse of the two parts and its time the two parts' time added (the rebuild timed to its solve, the spelling from its start). A fill-in is recorded when solved. Leaving mid-spelling resumes on the rebuild.
- A step whose word cannot be loaded leaves the stream unrated; a word failing twice ends the day's stream.

## Operations

- **onPlacePiece(id)** — fills the first open blank; filling the last blank checks the tray (no failed check is clean, one shaky, two or more failed)
- **onReturnPiece(blank)** / **onStartOver()** — empties one blank, or all
- **onShowNextPiece()** — puts the right piece into the first blank that is open or wrong (help: the step is failed)
- **onShowAnswer()** — fills every blank, which solves the step as failed
- **onPlaceLetter(id)** / **onReturnLetter(blank)** — the spelling's tiles; filling the last blank checks the word
- **onShowNextLetter()** / **onShowWord()** — the right letter into the first open or wrong blank, or the whole word (help: the spelling is failed)
- **onPickWord(wordId)** — picks a fill option
- **onShowPicture()** — uncovers a review's picture
- **onContinue()** — finishes a learning step (always allowed on `know`, otherwise once solved), starts a rebuild review's spelling once its rebuild is right ("Now spell it"), or leaves a review result
- **onRate(grade)** — replaces a review's derived rating; the card is rescheduled from its state before the review
- **onSaveNote(text)** — saves the word's note
- **onMeetMore()** — raises today's new-word allowance by 5 and goes back into the stream
- **readAloud(word)** — word, definition and example as one run
- **onPlayCard(word)** / **onPlayWord(word)** — the speakers: the whole card, or the word alone, with tap feedback
- **onTap()** — tap feedback for a press that only changes what is on screen
- **onPause()** — stops audio (the step stays saved)
