# Primary Button

## PrimaryButton(text, onClick, modifier, enabled, outlined)

The full-width pill for a screen's main action (Start, Continue, Now spell it, Done for today, Meet 5 more words): ink, or outlined in mist for the secondary one; dims while disabled, ripples and gives way under the finger (`pressMotion`).
