# Progress Tracker

Derives the counters Today shows from `daily_stats`.

## streak(history, today) -> consecutive days

Consecutive study days ending today (or yesterday — a day is not broken until it is over). The history may be in any order; a day counts when it met or reviewed a word or answered any graded step.

## greetingPeriod(hour) -> period

5-11 is morning, 12-17 afternoon, the rest evening.

## weeklyActivity(recentStats, today) -> seven days

The last seven days including today, oldest first, each with the graded steps answered that day; days without a row read as zero.

## heatmapData(recentStats, today, weeks?) -> HeatmapData

Heatmap data for the last 16 weeks by default, starting on a Monday. Each day's activity (graded steps answered) maps to an intensity of 0-4 by quarters of the largest day.

## estimatedDaysRemaining(remainingWords, recentStats) -> days or null

Days left at the average pace of the last (up to 14) days that met new words. 0 when nothing remains, null without data.

## mergeSession(existing, date, newLearned, reviewed, correctAnswers, totalAnswers) -> DailyStats

Folds counts into the day's row. Every field is a count, so merging is pure addition: a day folded step by step equals one folded at once.

## OverallProgress

Words in the release, words met, words being learned; remaining words and the share met (0 to 1) for the journey rail.
