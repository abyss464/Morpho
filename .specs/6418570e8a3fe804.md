# Glossed Text

English text whose gloss anchor words are marked with a quiet tint and underline; tapping one pops up its Chinese gloss. Product position: English carries the meaning, and the Chinese gloss is a last resort, like the picture.

## LocalGlossIndex

CompositionLocal that holds the current release's gloss_anchors index. Defaults to an empty index, so previews render plain English.

## GlossedText(text, style, color, modifier, underlines, underlineColor)

- **text** — AnnotatedString; its own styling (a bold headword) is kept
- **style** / **color** — text style and colour
- **underlines** — character ranges drawn with a 2dp rule in **underlineColor** just under the baseline (the headword in an example, the blank of the use step, the masked word of the spelling)
- With no anchor and no underline it is a plain Text with no extra overhead
- The popup shows the anchor's base form and its Chinese gloss, above the word (below when there is no room)
