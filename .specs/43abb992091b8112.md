# Step Parts

Pieces shared by the stream's step views.

## StageMark / StageLabel(mark, text, modifier)
The step label in the card's corner: a mist square for a new word, a copper diamond for learning steps, an ink diamond for reviews, then the label in tracked uppercase.

## WordPicture(word, modifier) / WidePicture(word, modifier)
The word's picture (its first example's image) clipped to the medium radius; without a picture, the word on the sunken ground. WidePicture is full width at 4:3.

## WordTitle(word, playing, onPlay, playLabel, modifier)
The headword in Garamond, its phonetic and part of speech, and a play button.

## DefinitionText(word, modifier, compact)
The primary definition with every form of the headword in semibold; on the copper tint, or plain and smaller when compact. Gloss anchors stay tappable.

## ExampleText(word, modifier)
The card example with its highlighted span in semibold over a copper rule.

## NoteBlock(note, modifier)
"In your own words" and the note, when there is one.

## WordCard(word, mark, label, note, playing, onPlay, modifier)
The full word card: picture, the label, word with phonetic, part of speech and the play-all speaker, definition, example and note. The `know` step and a looked-up word show it.

## Tone / StatusText(text, tone, modifier)
A one-line status in neutral, good or bad colour.

## PrimaryAction(text, onClick, modifier, enabled, outlined)
The full-width pill at the foot of a step; dims while disabled; outlined for the secondary action.

## CoveredPicture(modifier, content)
The sunken box with a "?" that hides a review's picture, holding its button.

## posLabel(pos)
"noun" -> "n.", "verb" -> "v.", and so on; anything else gets a trailing period.
