# Weekly Bar Chart

The last seven days as bars, one per day, by graded steps answered.

## WeeklyBarChart(activity, modifier)

- activity: daily activity, oldest first; the first seven items are drawn. Each item carries the date and the number of graded steps answered that day.

### Display

Bars on top, day-of-week letters beneath (M T W T F S S).

Bar height scales against the week's largest day; a day with nothing still shows a minimum stub. Today's bar and letter are copper, the rest muted mist; today's letter is bold.
