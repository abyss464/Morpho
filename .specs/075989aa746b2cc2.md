# Word Search

"Look up a word" on Today (docs/contracts/stream.md §7), matching the web client's look-up.

## SearchHit
A match: word id, spelling, and its unit.

## searchWords(index, query, limit) -> hits
The query trimmed and lowercased; an exact match first, then words starting with it, then words containing it; shorter words first within each, then learning order; at most 8.

## WordSearch(index, onPick, modifier)
A search field ("Look up a word") and, while there is a query, its matches under it with their units, or 'No word matches "…"'. Picking a match (or the keyboard's search key, for the first) clears the field and opens the word. The field and its matches scroll into view above the keyboard.
