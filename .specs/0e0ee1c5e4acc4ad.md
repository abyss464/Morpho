# Animation Duration Tokens

Animation durations in milliseconds, via `MorphoTheme.durations`:

- press (100) — press feedback scale; also the loader's stagger
- pulse (250) — half of one loader pulse
- shake (300) — horizontal shake on a wrong pick or failed check
- transition (300) — step and screen transition (shared X axis)
