# Review Result

A solved review (docs/contracts/stream.md §2, §6).

## ReviewResultContent(view, verdict, nowPlaying, viewModel)
Picture, "Review · Nth time", the word with a play button for the whole card, a verdict box tinted good / copper / bad by outcome ("Rebuilt with no mistakes", "Rebuilt after one slip", "Rebuilt with help", "Picked first time", "Picked after a miss") around the definition, the example, the note, the rating with "next review in …", the Again / Hard / Good / Easy control with each rating's interval (the chosen one tinted), "Rated from how you did. Tap another to change it.", and "Say it in your own words (optional)" — a field with Save (or the keyboard's Done).

## interval(days)
Days up to a month ("1 day", "22 days"), then months, then years.
