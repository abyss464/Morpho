# Today

The only way into learning (docs/contracts/stream.md §7). A vertical scroll: what the day holds and one button into the stream, then the long view.

## TodayScreen(container, onOpenStream, onOpenSettings, onOpenWord, modifier)

Shows Today and refreshes its figures every time it appears.

- container: the dependency container; used to create the ViewModel and play the tap sound
- onOpenStream: called by the Today card's button
- onOpenSettings: called by the settings button in the header
- onOpenWord: called with a word picked in "Look up a word"

Top to bottom:

1. **Greeting header** — greeting, wordmark, streak badge, settings
2. **Today card** — "Today", one line "R reviews and N new words" ("All done for today." when there is neither; a note that new words wait when reviews reach the backlog guard), and one full-width button: "Start" before the day's first step, "Continue" once a step was taken or one is waiting, "Today's summary" when nothing is left
3. **Journey** — words met of the release, the unit and the pace
4. **This week** — bar chart or heatmap (toggle on the section title)
5. **Look up a word** — the search field and its matches
6. **Units · progress** — "Finished X / U" and up to eight unit tiles around the first unfinished unit, two per row: "Unit N", "g/20" or "done", and a bar of graduated words. Units are progress only and open nothing.

## formatCount(value)

Formats an integer with thousands separators (5500 -> "5,500").

## ProgressTrack(fraction, modifier)

A 4dp rounded bar: the line colour as its track, ink as its fill.
