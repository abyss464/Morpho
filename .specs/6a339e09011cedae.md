# Done View

Today's stream is done (docs/contracts/stream.md §7).

## DoneView(summary, onDone, onMeetMore, modifier)
"Today's stream is done", "S steps, W words." (W = reviewed + met), three tiles — Reviewed (with how many had no mistakes), Met (with how many were explained and used first time), Tomorrow (reviews due by the end of tomorrow, plus the daily new words) — then the streak badge or "Come back tomorrow to start a streak.", and at the foot "Done for today" and "Meet 5 more words".
