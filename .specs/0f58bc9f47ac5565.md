# Rebuild Task

The explain step's controls (docs/contracts/stream.md §2).

## RebuildTray(state, onReturn, onStartOver, onShowNextPiece, modifier)
The dashed sunken tray the meaning is built in. Placed pieces return to the bank when tapped. After a failed check the misplaced pieces turn red and the tray shakes once; once solved the pieces and border turn green. Under it: the status ("Tap the pieces in order…", "Not quite…", "That's the meaning."), "Start over" while pieces are placed, and "Show the next piece" after a failed check.

## PieceBank(state, onPlace, modifier)
The pieces on offer, wrapped in rows. A piece in the tray keeps its slot here as an empty sunken shape, so nothing jumps. Hidden once solved.
