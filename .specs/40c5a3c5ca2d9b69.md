# Headword Marking

Finding the headword in running text (the domain's `wordPattern`), for bolding and for the use step's gap.

## markWord(text, word) -> AnnotatedString
Every form of the word in semibold.

## Marked / markExample(example, word)
The example with its highlighted span (or else the first form found) in semibold, and that span's range.

## Gap / gapOf(word)
The use step's sentence split around the word: the example's highlighted span; else the first form of the word in the example, then in the definition; else a bare blank.
