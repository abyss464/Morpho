# Rebuild Task

The explain step's controls (docs/contracts/stream.md §2, §4).

## RebuildTray(state, onReturn, modifier)
The definition as a flowing sentence in a dashed sunken tray: given text in place (word by word, punctuation tight against the blank before), open blanks as short rules — copper for the one the next piece fills, the strong line colour for the rest — and filled blanks as chips that go back to the bank when tapped, reopening their blank. After a failed check the wrong chips turn red and the tray shakes once; once solved the chips and the border turn green. Under it the status: "Tap pieces into the blanks in order. Some belong to other words.", "Not quite. Tap the red pieces to take them back." or "That's the meaning."

## PieceBank(state, onPlace, modifier)
The pieces on offer, wrapped in rows. A piece in a blank keeps its slot here as an empty sunken shape, so nothing jumps. Hidden once solved.

## RebuildActions(state, onShowNextPiece, onShowAnswer, onStartOver, modifier)
Under the bank until the tray is right: "Show the next piece", "Show the answer" and "Start over" (enabled once a blank is filled).
