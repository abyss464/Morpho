# Journey Progress

How much of the whole release the learner has met, as a card.

## JourneyProgress(overall, pace, modifier)

- overall: words in the release, words met (cards plus words in the stream), words being learned
- pace: the first unit not fully in review, the number of units, new words a day

### Card content

1. **Section title** "Journey"
2. **Main figure** — words met, in large Garamond, with "of N words" beside it
3. **Milestone rail** — quarter marks as the icon's diamonds: solid copper once passed, outlined while ahead
4. **Pace** — "Unit U of N · about D days at P a day", D = ceil(words not met / P); hidden once every word is met

## JourneyPace
The unit, the unit count and new words a day.
