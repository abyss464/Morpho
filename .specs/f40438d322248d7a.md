# Headword Marking

Finding the headword in running text, for bolding and for the use step's gap.

## wordPattern(word) -> Regex or null
Case-insensitive whole-word forms of the headword: the lemma, +s/es/ed/d/ing/'s; e-final stem + ing/ed; y-final stem + ies/ied; a doubled final b d g k l m n p r t + ed/ing. A multi-word headword matches with any run of spaces between its words. The web client's `wordPattern` follows the same rule.

## markWord(text, word) -> AnnotatedString
Every form of the word in semibold.

## Marked / markExample(example, word)
The example with its highlighted span (or else the first form found) in semibold, and that span's range.

## Gap / gapOf(word)
The use step's sentence split around the word: the example's highlighted span; else the first form of the word in the example, then in the definition; else a bare blank.
