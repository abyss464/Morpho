## Third-party text in release.db

Every example sentence and etymology in the current `release.db` is listed with its source,
a link and its licence in `content/attribution/examples.tsv` and
`content/attribution/etymology.tsv` (regenerated by `ops/attribution.py` on every release).

- **Tatoeba** (https://tatoeba.org): example sentences under CC BY 2.0 FR
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/) or CC0 1.0, as each row states. Each row
  links to the sentence, where its contributor is named.
- **Wiktionary** (https://en.wiktionary.org), fetched through the Free Dictionary API
  (https://dictionaryapi.dev): example sentences and etymologies under CC BY-SA 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Each row links to the entry. These rows
  stay under CC BY-SA, which allows commercial use under its own terms; the noncommercial
  restriction of this repository does not apply to them. Earlier releases in the git history
  also carry definitions from the same source under the same licence.
- **OpenSubtitles corpus** (OPUS, https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php; P. Lison and
  J. Tiedemann, 2016): 118 short example sentences, marked "no licence stated". They are
  included as brief quotations for language teaching and are to be replaced if a rights
  holder objects.

syllabus word list, the primary and junior-school syllabus lists, and COCA 20000 frequency
ranks); see `content/wordlists/README.md`.

## Not in this repository

Pictures and audio are not part of the repository. The pipeline fetches pictures from
Pixabay, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons (each under its own licence, recorded per picture
where the source reports it) and synthesizes speech locally; whoever builds and distributes
an APK with media is responsible for crediting those pictures.
