# Explain Puzzle

The "explain it" puzzle (docs/contracts/stream.md §4): the learner completes a word's primary definition by tapping pieces into its blanks in order. A faithful port of the web client's `explain.ts`: for the same word and pool the puzzle comes out the same, and the template with its answer pieces rebuilds the definition text exactly.

## Difficulty

EASY — pieces of at most 7 words, at most 6 blanks, 2 decoys; HARD — at most 4 words, 8 blanks, 3 decoys. One more decoy when the answer has 5 or more blanks.

## Piece / Segment / Puzzle

- **Piece** — an id and its text
- **Segment** — Given(text), shown in place, or Blank(index)
- **Puzzle** — the template in definition order, every piece on offer (the answer's plus decoys, shuffled), and for each blank the id of the piece that fills it

## Given text

The word itself in any form (`wordPattern`); each `;`-separated sense's opening when it names the word within its first 12 words (through a copula right after the word with an optional "also", or in an "If"/"When" sense to the clause's comma within 8 words; never the whole sense); prepositions and the first word of two-word prepositions; and closing punctuation. When nothing would be left to fill, prepositions become blanks.

## Blanks

Each run between given text is cut before clause words (that, who, which, …; not after "or"/"and"), after `,` `;` `:`, and nearest the middle when longer than the difficulty's word limit (preferring before "or"/"and"); one-word scraps fold into a neighbour. While there are too many blanks, the shortest pair separated only by prepositions (or nothing) merges, taking them in. A lone blank of four or more words splits in two.

## Pieces.puzzle(word, pool, difficulty) -> Puzzle
Builds the puzzle. Decoys are blank pieces of the pool words' primary definitions cut the same way, never equal to an answer piece, deduplicated and drawn by the word's seeded shuffle.

## Pieces.norm(text)
Trimmed, lowercased text, for comparing pieces.

## Seeded(seed)
The small deterministic generator (xorshift) the web client uses; `shuffle` is its Fisher-Yates.
